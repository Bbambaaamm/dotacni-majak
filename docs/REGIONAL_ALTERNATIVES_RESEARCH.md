# Regional public alternatives research — Středočeský / Vysočina

## Goal
Find safe official public coverage routes without bypassing protected grant portals.

### Středočeský kraj (#63)
Primary research target: public county site `stredoceskykraj.cz/web/dotace`.
The probe only enumerates same-origin public programme/document links.

### Kraj Vysočina (#71)
Primary research target: public Kraj Vysočina index tagged `Fond Vysočiny` on `kr-vysocina.cz`.
The protected `fondvysociny.cz` portal remains out of scope and is not bypassed.

## Safety
- no login
- no CAPTCHA bypass
- no cookies/auth logging
- no request/response bodies
- same-origin public link discovery only

The resulting connectors may explicitly expose **LIMITED coverage** when the official public alternative is not a complete catalogue.
